One probe-key grammar at every site that names one (§{cat.probes.decl},
§A.3.2 `BARE_PROBE_KEY`, CS-0201):

    PROBE_KEY ::= PROBE_SEG (":" PROBE_SEG)*
    PROBE_SEG ::= [A-Za-z_] [A-Za-z0-9_-]*

with a quoted form accepting any non-empty spelling. This fixture declares a
hyphenated key, a two-segment key, a three-segment key and a quoted key, then
consumes one through `ingredients` and seals the other three — the four
spellings and the three sites that used to disagree about them.
